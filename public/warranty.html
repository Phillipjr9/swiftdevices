What's Covered

  • Manufacturing defects — any fault in materials or workmanship that occurs under normal use.
  • Hardware component failure — screens, processors, memory, speakers, cameras, and charging ports.
  • Battery degradation — if battery health drops below 80% within the warranty period.
  • Software issues — pre-installed firmware or OS-related malfunctions.

What's Not Covered

  • Physical damage from drops, impacts, or liquid exposure (covered under SwiftCare+ only)
  • Damage caused by unauthorized modifications or repairs
  • Cosmetic damage such as scratches or dents that don't affect functionality
  • Damage from using non-approved accessories or chargers
