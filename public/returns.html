Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your product for any reason, you can return it within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund or exchange.

Eligibility

  • Product must be in its original packaging
  • All accessories, manuals, and cables must be included
  • Product must be in the same condition as received
  • Receipt or proof of purchase is required

How to Return

1

Contact Us

Email swiftdevices@technologist.com or call 1-800-SWIFT-01 with your order number.

2

Get Your Label

We'll email you a prepaid return shipping label within 24 hours.

3

Ship It Back

Pack the product securely, attach the label, and drop it off at any authorized shipping location.

4

Get Refunded

Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund will be processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Products with broken seals on software or digital content
  • Gift cards and downloadable products
  • Items damaged through misuse or accidental damage
